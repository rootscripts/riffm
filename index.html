Questions

Is Riff free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license.

Are there ads?

No. YouTube Music and SoundCloud play without ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Install Riff and press play. It does not ask you to sign in and does not collect any data.

Can it play my own music?

Yes. Riff plays FLAC, MP3, WAV and OGG, shows tags and covers, and lets you make playlists. Your files are not changed.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Downloaded tracks and your own files play without internet.

How do I import a playlist?

Paste a link to a public playlist from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Yandex Music, VK Music, Deezer, TikTok or Last.fm, or add a JSON file. Riff adds it to your library, even if it has more than 100 tracks.

Is there an Android or iOS app?

Not an official one. On phones the download button opens a page with community versions.

Are those phone versions official?

No. I did not make them. The page only lists ones I trust or that people sent me, so use any other version at your own risk.
